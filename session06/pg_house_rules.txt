============================================================
           PGFINDER HOSTEL & PG - HOUSE RULES
============================================================
1. Gate Closing Time: The main entry gate closes strictly at 10:30 PM. Late entry requires prior warden approval.
2. Visitor Policy: Outside guests and day visitors are allowed only in the common visitor lounge between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM. No visitors inside student rooms.
3. Quiet Hours: 11:00 PM to 6:00 AM are observed as mandatory quiet hours. Loud music or group discussions during this time are strictly prohibited.
4. Cleanliness: Residents must keep their individual rooms and common bathrooms neat and tidy. Daily garbage must be placed in designated bins.
5. Electrical Appliances: High-wattage heating rods, induction cooktops, and electric heaters are strictly forbidden inside bedrooms.
6. Meal Timings: Breakfast (7:30 AM - 9:30 AM), Lunch (12:30 PM - 2:30 PM), Dinner (7:30 PM - 9:30 PM). Mess timings must be respected.
7. Substance Ban: Smoking, alcohol consumption, and any prohibited substances are strictly banned on the entire PG premises. Zero tolerance policy applies.
8. Rent & Dues: Monthly PG rent must be cleared on or before the 5th day of every calendar month.
9. Security & Belongings: Residents are responsible for securing their personal belongings, laptops, and cash. Always lock your room when leaving.
10. Notice Period: A minimum of 30 days prior written notice is mandatory before vacating the PG to facilitate deposit refunds.
============================================================
